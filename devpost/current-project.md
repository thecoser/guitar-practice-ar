# Guitar Practice AR

*Your practice, within reach.*

Learning a song is easier when you can keep your eyes near your guitar. Guitar Practice AR is a SPECS Lens that puts current and upcoming chord shapes, lyrics, and timing cues beside the instrument. Set a comfortable tempo, position the chart, and play along with an adjustable backing band and a wordless melody guide.

Choose a built-in song, loop any chord changes in Chord practice, or import a full chord-and-lyric chart and save it to My Songs. The desktop demo shows the complete workflow with Home on the Range and an original sample chart, Morning Light.

The guitar and hands in Lens Studio Preview are a simulated player view. On SPECS, you see your own guitar and hands through the glasses as you play. You position the virtual chord chart beside your instrument so you can glance at the chord shapes, lyrics, and timing cues while keeping both hands on the guitar. The fingering and timing cues update live with the music.

The MVP uses a macOS helper to scan a printed page, then the Lens lets you review and save the imported chart. The intended release flow begins with scanning the page through the glasses, followed by the same in-Lens review and save.

Built from the Lens Studio Base Template with Devpost Learn planning, CLAD, Lens Studio, and Lens Studio MCP. The project uses Snap-provided packages and Preview scenery, generated guitar and branding artwork, traditional starter material with locally synthesized arrangements, and original narration and logo audio.
