## Draft Devpost description

When you’re learning guitar, looking away from your fretting hand to check a chord chart can cost you the next beat. Guitar Chord Loop puts a current and upcoming fingering beside the neck in SPECS. Preview Em, then follow a four-beat count-in and practice Em–G–C–D at 60 or 45 BPM. The metronome sets the pace, and the Next chart gives you a full bar to get ready. In Lens Studio Preview, the chart stayed fixed in the room after confirmation. The side-by-side layout kept both chord names visible during a downward glance and small lean, leaving the fretting hand clear.

The [Preview results](preview-results.md) record the layout and placement evidence and the remaining audio and device checks.

## Pre-recording rehearsal

1. Commit the candidate locally, then check it out into a separate clean folder and record the commit ID. With the original project closed, open the new path and follow its README. Verify every asset, package, font, click and preview fixture loads from the candidate or the standard Lens Studio installation. Inspect project references for absolute paths back to the original workspace.
2. From reset run one full loop at 60 and one at 45 with count-in, all chord changes and wraparound. Confirm framing, visible Next and audible clicks. Fix any issue in the source, take a new clean checkout and repeat.
3. Record the rehearsed build. Verify both system audio and screen capture in the output before filming the full take. Keep clicks audible under narration, avoid edits across beat intervals, and check the final export for label and diagram readability. Use the original metronome and your own or cleared guitar performance.
4. Have a first-time viewer watch without opening the project. Ask them who it helps, what they saw, and how placement and beat timing solve the problem. Fix and recheck if any answer is unclear.
5. Entrant verifies YouTube public playback and actual judge repository access, confirms links in Devpost, and submits before the deadline.
